[h2]How to unlock forms[/h2]

Potential Unleashed (Legendary trait cant access this form):
1.Craft Z Sword
((    Hallowed Bar - 20     ))
((    Gold Bar - 10         ))
((    Hell Stone Bar - 15   ))
((    Soul of Sight - 5     ))
((    Soul of Might - 5     ))
((    Soul of Fright - 5    ))
2.Hit Golem with it
3.Talk to Elder Kai after breaking sword
(You also should have ssj2 unlocked)

Beast (Only for Prodigy):
Beat Moonlord with mastered Ultimate 

[h2]Forms Multipliers[/h2]

Ultimate / Potential Unleashed:

Damage: +250% | Speed: +225%
Defense: +19  | Ki Costs: +55%
Ki Drain: 240/s, 120/s when mastered

Beast:

Damage: +360%(+40% From Charge) | Speed: +400%
Defense: +49  | Ki Costs: +100%
Ki Drain: 300/s, 162/s when mastered
